# Customer Support and Escalation

## Support Topics

Customers can ask about:
- delivery areas
- delivery windows
- policy guidance
- payment methods
- general subscription guidance
- order concerns that need owner follow-up

## Escalation

Escalate to the owner when:
- the customer is frustrated
- the issue needs a manual exception
- live order data is required
- a policy question is unresolved
- a payment or delivery dispute needs review
