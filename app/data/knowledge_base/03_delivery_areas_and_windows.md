# Delivery Areas and Windows

## Delivery Areas

TiffinAI serves selected neighborhoods in Lahore and nearby business districts.

Examples of commonly served areas:
- Gulberg
- DHA Lahore
- Johar Town
- Model Town
- Faisal Town
- Garden Town

If an area is not listed, the customer should be asked to confirm the address through the business workflow.

## Delivery Windows

- Breakfast: 7:00 AM to 9:00 AM
- Lunch: 12:30 PM to 1:30 PM
- Dinner: 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM

Delivery windows can shift slightly on busy days or public holidays. The live order workflow remains the source of truth for a specific order.
