# Operating Hours

## Customer Support Hours

- Monday to Saturday: 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM
- Sunday: 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM

## Kitchen Preparation Window

- Breakfast preparation starts early morning.
- Lunch preparation starts late morning.
- Dinner preparation starts in the afternoon.

Exact live delivery timing for a specific order is managed by the order workflow and not by the knowledge base.
