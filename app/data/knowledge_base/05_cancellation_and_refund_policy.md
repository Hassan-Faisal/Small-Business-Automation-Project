# Cancellation and Refund Policy

## Cancellations

- Meal and order cancellation requests are reviewed against the business rules.
- Very late cancellation requests may not be accepted.
- If the customer asks to cancel, the assistant should route the request to the live workflow or owner follow-up path.

## Refunds

- Refund eligibility depends on the order state and timing.
- The assistant must not promise a refund without checking the live order record and the business policy.
- Approved refunds are handled manually or through the business workflow.
