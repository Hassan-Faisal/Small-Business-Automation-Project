# Frequently Asked Questions

## What should I do if I want today's menu?
Ask for today's menu through the ordering workflow. The live menu comes from the database.

## Can I ask about delivery timing?
Yes. General delivery hours can be answered from policy documents.

## Can I ask about my current order?
No. Live order status must come from the order workflow.

## Can I ask about my subscription?
No. Customer-specific subscription information must come from the subscription workflow.

## Can I ask about ingredients or allergens?
Yes. General allergen guidance is available, but exact live confirmation may require owner review.
