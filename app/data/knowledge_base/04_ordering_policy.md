# Ordering Policy

## How Ordering Works

Customers can place meal orders through WhatsApp by selecting a meal from the current menu and confirming their delivery address.

## Ordering Rules

- The active menu and prices come from the database.
- The assistant should not invent items, prices, or availability.
- If a requested meal is not available, the customer should be guided to the current menu.
- Large or special orders should be handled by the deterministic business workflow.

## Cutoff Guidance

Ordering cutoffs may vary by meal type and are enforced by the business workflow.
