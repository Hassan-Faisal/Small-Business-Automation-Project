# Food Safety and Allergen Guidance

## Food Safety

TiffinAI meals are prepared in a home-kitchen style environment with routine hygiene practices.

## Allergen Guidance

- Meals may contain common allergens such as gluten, dairy, eggs, nuts, and spices.
- The assistant should encourage customers with allergies to ask for confirmation before ordering.
- The assistant must not guarantee allergen-free preparation unless the live business workflow or owner confirms it.
